Lesson 01 // Introduction

Setting Up: Installing VS Code

01
Download

Visit code.visualstudio.com. The site will automatically detect your Operating System.

Windows macOS Linux
02
Install

Run the installer. Follow the prompts to set up the application on your local hard drive.

Windows User? Check "Add to PATH" to enable terminal commands later.
03
Launch

Open VS Code. You should see a "Welcome" tab. Pin the icon to your Taskbar or Dock for easy access.

Ready! Your digital workshop is now open for business.

Presenter Notes

Teaching Intent: Guide students through the physical setup of their environment. This is often where beginners feel the most anxiety, so keep it simple.

Explanation: 1. Direct them to the official website: code.visualstudio.com. Emphasize that it's free and maintained by Microsoft. 2. Mention that the site is smart and will show the correct download button for their computer. 3. For Step 2, specifically mention the "Add to PATH" checkbox for Windows users. It's a common stumbling block later when they try to use the command line. 4. For Mac users, remind them to drag the app into the 'Applications' folder. 5. Once they launch it, they've successfully crossed the first technical hurdle.

Transition: "Now that we have the workshop open, let's stock it with some specialized tools that will make our lives much easier."

Animation Suggestion: The three cards will fade in together to maintain focus on the sequence.
