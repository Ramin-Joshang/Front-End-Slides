Presenter Notes

Now, let's look at the actual conversation. This is called the HTTP Request-Response cycle. Think of it like ordering at a restaurant. Your browser is the customer (the Client). It sends a "Request" saying, "I'd like the index.html file, please." The Server is the waiter. It receives that request, goes into its storage, and sends back a "Response."

In our animation, you can see the blue 'GET' request moving to the server. The green response then travels back with the content. But the response isn't just the file; it includes a "Status Code."

  • 200 OK: The gold standard. "I found it, here you go."
