One Root Folder
Keep every project in its own self-contained directory. Never build directly on your desktop.
Lowercase Naming
Use lowercase letters and hyphens. Avoid spaces and special characters to prevent broken links.
Logical Subfolders
Group files by type (CSS, JS, Images) to keep the project scalable as it grows.
Tip: Your main page must always be named index.html
